# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- Demo: the view controls' Auto-turn speeds are the family's speed buttons, 0.5×, 1× and 2× (kazoku-ui 0.14, `^0.14.0`), in place of Slow, Medium and Fast. The reader's choice is the controls' `pace` attribute and `?pace=2` in the address (the address key was `speed`). The base speed stays `speed="5"` degrees a second, which the buttons multiply, and Turn left and Turn right keep their way at any speed. The copied embed code turns at the same speed as the page (`spin` is the seconds a turn: 144, 72 or 36), where it said `slow` whatever the buttons said. Browser tests hold the three buttons, the speed each sets (sign kept), the address and the copied code, and that an old `?speed=fast` opens at 1×.
- Fixed: the drawing of a flight (no WebGL, `mode="drawing"`, the embed page before the 3D view) took each phase's own angles for the grid fins, legs and flaps, so every move jumped at a phase while the 3D view mixed them. It now takes the angles the 3D view has at that moment, rounded to 5 degrees: the drawing changes at most about one time in five degrees of a move (17 times for Falcon 9's grid fins opening from 85 degrees), still no more than about 20 draws a second. A browser test seeks across the fins' opening and holds that each angle has its drawing, the drawing changes only when the rounded angle does, and the phase's own drawing is no longer the one for all of it; it fails without the fix.
- Documentation: the Element page, the Looks guide and the Upgrading guide (English and Japanese) say what the speed buttons do; the README's pictures of the demo show them.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-03

- Fixed: a look chosen while the 3D view starts, or while a rocket loads, is no longer lost. `start()` and the rocket change read the look before the load and applied it after, so the attribute said the new look and the view drew the old one; both now read it again after the load (and the parts shown with it). Two browser tests change the look in the turn the canvas appears and again with a variant change, and fail without the fix.
- Fixed: `data-painted` no longer stays at `loading` after the painted look is left. Leaving it now sets `data-painted` to an empty value and sends no `uchusen-painted` event for it. A browser test holds a model back, chooses painted, then line.
- Where the browser can build shaders in parallel (the `KHR_parallel_shader_compile` extension, which real graphics cards have), the first draw of a look waits for three.js's `compileAsync`, so the page keeps answering while a look is first built and the last picture stays up meanwhile (a model that comes in later builds its own the same way, with a four-second limit so a browser that never says it is ready still draws; `data-painted` says `model` or `code` only once they are built). Software drawing, which every test here uses, has no such extension, so nothing waits there and it behaves as before. Measured in software on a Mac with and without the wait forced on: choosing the painted look held the page for tasks of 206 and 427 ms before, and 204 alone or 192 and 412 ms after (which of the two depends on whether the model has arrived before the first draw), so software shows no gain, as expected; the gain on a real graphics card is not measured, because no test may draw on one.
- Falcon 9 v1.0 with Dragon 1 (`v1.0-dragon`), the vehicle that flew COTS Demo Flight 1: the v1.0's two stages with Dragon 1 on top and no fairing (47.8 m with Dragon 1 as the Falcon 9 v1.0 article's infobox states it; the parts are drawn to more and the variant says so). A flight for it, and a flight for Dragon 1 on its own, from SpaceX's COTS Demo Flight 1 press kit (its mission timeline, page 7) and Spaceflight Now's launch timeline, both written before the launch, so the plan: liftoff, max q at 1:16, MECO at 2:58, separation at 3:02, second stage ignition at 3:09, cutoff at 9:00 in a "186-mile-high orbit", Dragon's separation at 9:35 (the trunk stays with the second stage, as the timeline says), then Dragon's deorbit burn at 2:32 (hours and minutes), entry at 2:58, drogue parachutes at "45,000 feet", main parachutes at "about 10,000 feet" and the water landing at 3:19. The two engines that shut down early have no time on the page, so that step has none. Dragon's thrusters and parachutes are not drawn.
- The flight bar takes `words`: `flight.words = { play: "再生" }` says its own words (Play, Pause, Back, Next, the slider's name, the note that there is no timeline and the lines of its source note) in another language; what is left out stays English, and a phase's name and what happens in it are the data's.
- Demo: the whole page is in Japanese when asked (`?lang=ja` or the language picker), not only its header and footer: the choices, the facts table's labels, the embed and compare sections, the builder, the parts, what is planned and the sources, with the flight bar's words. The words are `demo/words.js`, English and Japanese side by side, and a test holds that each key is in both with the same blanks. Notes, quotations and flight steps that come from a source stay as the source gave them, in English, and the page says so. The Japanese awaits a native reader. A browser test opens the page in Japanese, checks it and switches back by the picker.
- Demo: the test that ignored the touch icon's published address is gone, now that the family's icon link is relative.

The flight bar shows a speed beside the altitude.
- Grid fins, legs and flaps turn in a flight. Each is a group of pieces with a name, a pivot and an axis (`Piece.move`), a node of its own in the `.glb` (`booster~grid-fins~0`, with its axis), turned in the 3D view by setting one rotation and in the drawing by `moved` (`svg({ moves })`); `PartPose.moves` carries the angles a phase wants and `frameAt` mixes them. A painted model that is one shell does not turn: nothing is cut to fake a move. No new draw loop: a turn is set inside the flight's one frame, and the number of nodes, buffers and programs is the same each time (the freeze test holds it).

- The family's must-have list passes (`node scripts/check-standard.mjs` says ok, and a test holds the file to its recorded hash): the demo is made with the family's header and footer (the language picker and the theme switch, Docs and Embed links, light and dark), the family's template and README writer list this package, the README has "The family", and the documentation site has its recorded-hash test and its browser test.

- A flame's colour comes from what its engine burns, each from a page read for it: `PROPELLANTS` and `propellantOf` (Merlin, Kestrel, F-1, Rutherford and the RD-107 and RD-108 burn kerosene; Raptor and BE-4 methane; J-2, RS-25 and BE-3 hydrogen; the Space Shuttle's boosters and the SLS's are solid; the lunar module's descent engine burns Aerozine 50 and nitrogen tetroxide). A part with an engine no page read here names, such as the ship of the 2019 design and of Block 4, has a neutral grey-white flame. Each part has its own, in the drawing and the 3D view (`BuiltPart.flame`), and a Space Shuttle's orbiter and boosters burn two colours at once. No new draw: the flames of a kind share one program and one set of clocks, and the freeze test passes. The colours are a picture.

Both now name `three/addons/`, as the snippet the demo copies always did. `pnpm test:usage` found it.
- WebKit's "Refused to apply a stylesheet" errors, which the demo test had been told to ignore, are gone and the ignore is removed. They came from the demo page's Content-Security-Policy after the page lost its style hash (chikyu-earth 0.10's realistic Earth then fell back to a style tag the policy refused). ## [0.2.1] - 2026-11-02

- Fixed: the demo could hang the screen while turning. When the 3D view turned by itself, or glided to a stop after a drag, the controls' change event asked for the next frame while the view's own loop did too, so each frame started two and the number of draws doubled every frame: thousands of draws a second, enough to starve the graphics card (measured in a real browser on a real graphics card: 12,308 draws in 3 seconds, against 181 now at 60 frames a second). One frame is asked for, once.
- Fixed: every change of variant, look or parts left the old shapes in the graphics card (about 60 buffers each), and a painted model's pictures with them. They are given back now, and the glow's too.
- Fixed: the canvas had no size limit but a ratio of 2. It is held to 4096 pixels a side and 6 million pixels in all, so a view as wide as a 5K screen is no longer drawn at 15 million.
- The drawing, with no WebGL, is zoomed and moved by its `viewBox` instead of a CSS scale, so it never grows past its box; it is made again at most 20 times a second, never while out of sight (a hidden tab, scrolled away), and is not rebuilt when only the zoom or the move changes.
- The "Tilt to turn" listener is let go when the 3D view is, and does nothing out of sight; it was added again after each return to the screen.
- `drive()` with the same choice again does nothing.
- The demo's auto-turn stops after a minute and when the view leaves the screen; Play starts it again.
- `pixelRatioFor(width, height, deviceRatio)`, with `MAX_PIXELS`, `MAX_SIDE` and `MAX_RATIO`.
- New browser tests: draws per frame, nothing drawing when still or out of sight, no growth of contexts, buffers, vertex arrays or textures over changes, a lost context, and the canvas within its budget from 1x to 5x on a 5K screen.
