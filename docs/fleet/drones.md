# Yard drones

The five machines are meshes built in this package, not imported models.

The repair saucer follows the form of a concept image supplied for this work: a white saucer with an orange rim, a dark underside, a raised centre, a lens at the front with a gold ring, orange housings beside the lens, two antennae and two arms underneath. The artist is not named, and no page address was given with the image, so there is no source URL to record. Logos, flags and lettering on that image are left out. The livery is this package's own.

The welder, the carrier, the tug and the fitter are still the earlier meshes. Each is to be brought to the form of its own supplied picture the same way.
