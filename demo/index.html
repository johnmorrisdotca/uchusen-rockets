Rockets made by code from sourced sizes. Pick a variant, take it apart, turn it round. Every figure names its source, and a blank means no source gives it.

Drag to turn it. The controls tip, zoom and move it; Ctrl or Command with the wheel zooms.

Build one from parts

Every part has the same record: its size, its origin and the joints it mates at, so a part from anywhere can go in a slot. The check says whether the joints fit, and a mix is labelled as what it is: never a vehicle that flew.
